Award & Recognition

StemLearn Wins Frontier Ingenuity/Innovation Prize 🏆

Awarded for outstanding creativity in technical and logical design and honoured with a special prize from Frontier, proud sponsors of Robocon 🚀

An outstanding performance by the StemLearn team, their robot truly captured everyone’s attention. A remarkable piece of engineering by school students, showcasing creativity, precision, and incredible teamwork under pressure.

The robot’s eye and its movement were a real highlight, a brilliant example of innovation meeting imagination 🤖✨

Well done, team, a fantastic achievement! 🚀

From Robotics Champions to Space Explorers! STEM Learn Beyond Limits!

Another outstanding achievement for STEM Learn!

Following their outstanding performance at RoboCon, our talented young team has proudly achieved Flight Status in the European Space Agency (ESA) & Astro Pi – Mission Space Lab Challenge, an inspiring opportunity where students apply mathematics, computing, and real ISS data to solve authentic space science problems.

Mission Space Lab (astro-pi.org)

In partnership with the European Space Agency and Astro Pi, this achievement reflects true dedication, innovation, and a deep passion for STEM.

👏 Huge congratulations to our students, your journey is turning ideas into real space impact. This is only the beginning!
